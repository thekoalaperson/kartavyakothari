01 — whoami

A koala who happens to do machine learning.

Today I'm a Principal Machine Learning Scientist at PlayVisionAI, a stealth sports-video AI startup in Mumbai. I build the primitives that turn footage into tracks, events, highlights, and player insights, working across representation learning, multimodal models, evaluation, and efficient video processing.

At Dream Play (Dream Sports), I built ML for a global padel product: ratings and personalized technical, tactical, and physical feedback. With sports scientists, I formulated a skill score from 60+ detected metrics and built distributed pipelines for insights, highlights, and leaderboards.

Before that I spent four years at Dream11, India's largest fantasy sports platform, growing from Data Scientist to Senior and then Lead ML Scientist. I built and owned personalization & experimentation across gameplay and lifecycle at national scale (30M+ MAU, ~15M concurrent at peak): contest recommendation, smart discounting, bundling, and real-time fraud detection that quietly shaped a stadium-sized crowd every match day.

I came up through IIT Bombay (M.Tech, ML & NLP), researching multilingual knowledge graphs under Prof. Soumen Chakrabarti. I've stayed close to research ever since, co-authoring papers at KDD, ECML-PKDD and AI-ML Systems. I like the part where rigorous ideas meet real users at uncomfortable scale.

The koala thing? Long story. Short version: I'd rather stay in, learn deeply, and think in weird analogies until the unconventional solution falls out, then ship it. 🌿
